Study parameters

The three geometry parameters of the self-heating study. All lengths are in micrometres (1 nm = 0.001 µm). Every other coordinate in the structure is recomputed from these.

µm Vertical (Y) extent of each sheet. Drives sheet pitch, gate bands and device height.
µm Transverse (Z) extent of each sheet. Drives the HfO2 collars, gate bridges and the pMOS offset.
µm Si3N4 wall between nMOS and pMOS. Sets the device separation and the well split plane.
