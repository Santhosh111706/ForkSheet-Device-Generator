Study parameters

The primary geometry parameters. All lengths are in micrometres (1 nm = 0.001 µm). Every other coordinate in the structure is recomputed from these, and from the design constants and doping below.

µm
Vertical (Y) extent of each sheet. Drives sheet pitch, gate bands and device height.
µm
Transverse (Z) extent of each sheet. Drives the HfO2 collars, gate bridges and the pMOS offset.
sheets
Sheets per transistor. Every dependent band, spacer piece, collar and doping placement is rebuilt from this. The structured V8 formats are written for 3; other counts emit step-by-step.
µm
Si3N4 wall between nMOS and pMOS. Sets the device separation and the well split plane.

Generation options

One at a time varies each parameter while holding the other two at the values above. Full grid takes every combination.
comma separated
auto keeps sweep runs from overwriting each other. A fixed name such as fork1108 matches an existing sdevice.cmd Grid entry.
writes <prefix>_msh.tdr
